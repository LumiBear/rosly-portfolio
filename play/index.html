Side quests.

Case studies are where I solve problems for people. This is where I play: type experiments, posters, and small projects I build for the fun of it.

Graphic design

Brand identity, posters, social and product graphics. These are projects I designed on my own, outside my UX case studies.

Brand identity · Social · Print

Guppy Nailz

Launch brand for a small nail business, built around the owners’ love for their cat Guppy and a cute scrapbook theme. Includes an Instagram post, a poster, instructions card and business cards.

Infographic · Poster

White Tiger

An infographic on the idolization of the exotic white tiger, made to draw a parallel in life while raising awareness.

Product marketing

DaintyDeskCo

Etsy listing images, feature graphics and a shop banner for a small shop that sells K-pop Notion planners.

Logo · Brand identity

Bloom & Root

A solo logo project for an emerging plant-based café in Austin, Texas. The brief asked for something fresh and approachable, not clinical or preachy, so I paired a forest-green and warm-sand palette with Etna and Quicksand.

Poster · Campaign

Poster concepts

Two posters. On the left, an unofficial student concept for a cherry cola lip tint, not affiliated with Fwee. On the right, an awareness poster that points people to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Website design

Froth

A website redesign pitch for a local coffee shop, aimed at college students. Home, menu and Work with us pages.

Typography · Poster series

MUNCH

A typography series built from a funny Craigslist post. I pushed one word, MUNCH, through rough, ruffled and clean letterforms, then let the ad copy for Mr. Pigglesworth’s Garden Service crowd in around it.
