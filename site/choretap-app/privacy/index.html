Privacy Policy

Chore Tap is a small household-chores app made by Rosly as a student design project. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does with your information.

The short version: there are no accounts. Everything you enter stays on your own phone or browser. We do not collect it, send it anywhere, sell it or show ads.

What the app stores

  • Your first name and an optional profile photo.
  • Your house: its name, housemates, stickers, chore history and settings.
  • Anything you type on the guest page, such as a Wi-Fi name and password or house rules. Please only enter things you are comfortable sharing with your guests.

All of this is saved in your device's own storage. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

What you can share

If you tap Send invite or share a guest link, the house details inside that link go to whoever you send it to. The link itself carries the information, so only share it with people you trust.

Notifications and NFC

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device. Tapping an NFC sticker or scanning a QR code opens a link and logs the chore on your device. We never see your location or when you open the app.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Your profile → Start over to erase everything the app has stored on your device. Clearing your browser's site data does the same.

Children

Chore Tap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated policy.
