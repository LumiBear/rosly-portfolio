Privacy Policy

Chore Tap is a small household-chores app made by Rosly as a student design project. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app uses and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Your password: if you sign up with email, it is handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase) and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot read it.
  • Your house: the house name and code, the stickers (chores) you set up, the people in the house, and the activity feed of who logged what and when.
  • Optional extras: a profile photo, and anything you type on the guest page (for example a Wi-Fi name and password or house rules). Please only enter things you are comfortable sharing with the people you give that page to.
  • On your device: settings such as sound, notification and theme preferences, and a copy of your house so the app works offline.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, show your house to your housemates, keep everyone's chore log in sync, and remind you when a chore is due. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data to track you across other websites or apps.

Who can see it

  • Your housemates can see your name, your photo if you added one, and the chores you log in that house.
  • Guests who open a guest-page link can see only what you put on that page.
  • Nobody else can read your house. Access is limited to signed-in members of that house.

Services that help us run the app

  • Supabase stores accounts and house data and handles sign-in.
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Google provides optional "Continue with Google" sign-in.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

NFC stickers and your camera or sensors

When you tap a sticker, the app reads a short link stored on the sticker. The link contains only the chore's ID and name, never personal information. Chore Tap does not use your location or contacts.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account any time in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your sign-in and removes you from your houses. Your name may remain in a house's past activity history until the other housemates clear it. To have anything removed sooner, or to ask what data we hold about you, email us at the address below.

Children

Chore Tap is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so only house members can read a house. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated policy.
