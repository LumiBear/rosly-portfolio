Graphic design

Brand identity, posters, social and product graphics. These are projects I designed on my own, outside my UX case studies.

Social · Print

Guppy Nailz

Launch brand for a small nail business, built around the owners’ love for their cat Guppy and a cute scrapbook theme. Includes a three-slide Instagram carousel (restock, how to order, how to pick up) and a poster with tear-off QR tabs.

An infographic on the idolization of the exotic white tiger, made to draw a parallel in life while raising awareness. A two-page spread with a halftone portrait, plus the blackletter title treatment.

Logo · Brand identity

Bloom & Root

A solo logo project for an emerging plant-based café in Austin, Texas. The brief asked for something fresh and approachable, not clinical or preachy, so I paired a forest-green and warm-sand palette with Etna and Quicksand.
